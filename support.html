Measuring

Point the camera at the base of the object, not its top. The distance is computed to where the aiming line meets the ground, so aiming at the foot of a tree, post or building gives the right number. The angle readout turns green when the tilt is good.

Method labels. LIDAR is the phone's laser rangefinder, exact to a centimetre up to 6 m. DEPTH covers 6 to 15 m from the camera image. TERRAIN uses elevation data along the aiming line and is best from 50 m upwards. GPS means no terrain data was available and the number comes from angle and height alone. MAP is a distance between points you placed on the map.

GPS ONLY badge. The elevation services could not be reached, usually because there is no data connection. The app keeps measuring by angle; terrain returns when the connection does.

INDOOR badge. GPS and compass are too weak for terrain ranging, so only LiDAR and DEPTH are used. Long-press the info bar, or tap the AUTO chip, to force indoor or outdoor mode.

COMPASS badge. The heading is uncertain. Move the phone in a figure of eight away from metal, or step away from cars and railings.

Map mode and lines

Tap the round viewfinder button to switch to the map. Long-press once to place A, long-press again to place B, and the line shows its length. Drag a pin to move it. Tap the label on a line to name it, save it or delete it. Free keeps one line; ViewRange Pro keeps as many as you draw and adds them up into a path.

ViewRange Pro

Pro is a one-time purchase, not a subscription, and it is shared with your family through Family Sharing. It adds more than one line with labels and a path total, directions to the measured target in Apple Maps or Google Maps, 5x digital zoom, satellite and hybrid map styles, a persistent history with notes and photos, and annotated pictures without a watermark. The free app keeps every measurement source at full accuracy.

Restore a purchase on a new device: open the ⓘ About screen and tap Restore Purchases, or use the same button on the paywall. You must be signed in with the Apple Account that bought Pro or a member of its family group.

Offer codes. Open ⓘ About, tap Redeem Offer Code and enter the code, or open the link you were sent.

Saving and pictures

Save reading keeps the current distance in History. Save picture writes an annotated photo to your Photos library; the first time, iOS asks for permission to add photos. If you declined, allow it under Settings › ViewRange › Photos.

Accuracy

LiDAR is exact to about 1 cm. Terrain ranging depends on GPS accuracy, compass accuracy and the elevation data of the area; expect a few percent at a few hundred metres. Steep slopes, tall buildings in front of the target and a weak compass widen the error. The number is never adjusted to look better than the data allows.
