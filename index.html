Measure the distance to what you can see.

A rangefinder for iPhone and iPad. Point the camera, read the distance.

ViewRange combines the phone's GPS, compass, motion sensors, LiDAR and public elevation data to measure distances from a centimetre up to many kilometres, with the method and its uncertainty shown next to every number. Nothing is uploaded to a server of ours; the app works on the device and asks public elevation services only for the ground heights it needs.

Free measures with every source at full accuracy, one map line, 2x zoom and three readings per session.
ViewRange Pro, a one-time purchase shared with your family, adds unlimited lines with labels and a path total, directions to the target, 5x zoom, satellite and hybrid maps, persistent history with notes and photos, and clean annotated pictures.
